About Our Apps

We build highly customizable, productivity-focused browser extensions designed to improve your workflow, privacy, and user experience. Everything operates locally on your machine by default.

Our Extensions

Zero Startpage

A highly customizable Chrome extension that replaces your default new tab page with a clean, aesthetic, and productivity-focused interface.

  • Smart Search: Fast Google text search, Google Lens visual search, and quick access to Google Apps.
  • Dynamic Clock & Date: Real-time clocks with multiple layouts (minimal, analog, prism) and countdown integrations.
  • Productivity Tools: Built-in Todo list, Habit Tracker, Pomodoro Timer, Notepad, and Full Calendar (with public holiday support).
  • Media & Entertainment: Mini Music Player, RSS Feed reader, and Daily Quotes.
  • Extreme Customization: Personalize wallpapers, fonts, colors, and layout positioning.

Zero Bookmark Manager

A powerful and intuitive extension that simplifies bookmark organization, allowing you to view, search, sort, and manage your bookmarks with a sleek interface.

  • Multiple Views: Browse bookmarks via Flat, Tree, Detail, Card, List, Bento, Kanban, and Mockup views.
  • Advanced Search & Sort: Instantly find bookmarks with fuzzy search, and sort by date, alphabet, or domain.
  • Sidebar & Folder Management: Raindrop-style sidebar with nested folder tree and Drag & Drop support.
  • Web Preview: Open an Iframe Modal to preview websites without leaving the extension.
  • Link Health Check: Verify the availability and safety of your bookmarked links.

Why We Request User Data

We believe in full transparency. Our extensions operate locally on your machine by default. However, to provide advanced cloud sync features across devices, our apps may request specific OAuth permissions from your Google Account.

Data Requested & Purpose:
  • Google Drive (drive.file): We request this scope so you can optionally backup, sync, and restore your personalized settings, layouts, and bookmarks directly to your own Google Drive. The app only has access to the specific configuration file it creates, ensuring your other Drive files remain completely private and inaccessible.
  • User Profile (userinfo.profile): We request basic profile information simply to identify your account and display your profile picture within the app's sync settings, confirming you are actively logged in.

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for tracking/advertising purposes. Authentication is strictly used to facilitate your personal cloud backups.
