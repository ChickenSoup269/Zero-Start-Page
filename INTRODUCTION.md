# Startpage

Startpage is a highly customizable Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a clean, feature-rich, and aesthetically pleasing interface. Everything is stored locally — no accounts, no tracking.

-----

Key Features:

Visuals & Backgrounds
- 57+ Live Animations & WebGL Shaders: Matrix Rain, Sakura Petals, Snowfall HD, Aurora Waves, Interactive Fluid simulation, Frosted Glass Orbs, Black Hole singularity, Neon Grid 3D, and more.
- Dynamic Backgrounds: Support for solid colors, gradients, custom images (URL/Upload), and background video loops (MP4/WebM).
- Free High-Resolution Photos: Instant curated wallpapers from LoremFlickr and Lorem Picsum with category filtering and instant save to local gallery.
- Unsplash Integration: Browse and set photos directly from Unsplash with custom API keys.
- SVG Wave Generator: Create, customize, and save unique vector wave backgrounds with multi-layer flow.

Productivity Suite
- Smart Search: Multi-engine search (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, Perplexity, Gemini, YouTube, GitHub) with real-time suggestions, Google Lens support, and quick access to Google Apps.
- Bookmark Manager: Organize bookmarks into customizable tab groups with independent colors and auto-fetching favicons, plus browser import with folder-to-group generation.
- Task Management: Minimalist Todo List and Notepad with rich text editing and resizable floating windows.
- Habit Tracker: Build daily routines with streak counters and customizable color palettes.
- Advanced Calendar: Full-month view with event management, Vietnamese Lunar Calendar (Âm lịch), and public holiday detection.
- Timer & Alarm: Smart countdown timer with quick-input parsing (`30` -> 30s, `130` -> 1:30) that persists after page reloads.
- Smart Weather: Real-time weather forecast via Open-Meteo with custom endpoints, geocoding, and connection tester.
- Daily Quotes & RSS Reader: Curated daily inspirational quotes and a lightweight multi-feed RSS news reader.

Music & Ambient Audio
- Global Music Player: Control media playing in any tab (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music) directly from your startpage.
- Audio Reactive Visualizers: Vinyl, Pixel EQ, and dynamic bars reacting to live tab audio beats in real-time.
- Ambient Sounds Generator: 12 high-definition white noise and nature sounds (Rain, Waves, Cafe, Forest, Campfire, etc.) with multi-track volume mixer and sleep timer.

Deep Customization
- 14+ Clock Styles: Including 3D Floating Glass Cubes, Fliqlo Flip, Analog, Pixel HUD & Satellite with custom palette, and 60 FPS GPU-optimized Glass Float.
- Widget Control: Freely drag, reposition (Free Move), snap to grid (20px to 100px), lock, or hide any component.
- Transparent Skin & Borderless Mode: Toggle transparent frosted aesthetics and borderless style across all widgets.
- Glassmorphism UI: A modern, frosted-glass aesthetic across all elements and dialogs.
- Font & Color Management: Use locally installed system fonts, Google Fonts, and dynamic Material 3 (M3) accent colors extracted from wallpapers or album art.
- Tab Personalization: Customize browser tab title and set custom emojis or icons as your favicon.
- Multi-Language Support (i18n): English, Vietnamese, German, and Swedish, featuring vector SVG country flags.

Privacy & Performance
- 100% Local: Your data (notes, todos, bookmarks, habits) is stored exclusively on your device (IndexedDB & LocalStorage). No accounts, no tracking.
- Lightweight: Built with pure Vanilla JavaScript (ES Modules) for maximum speed (<100ms load) and zero runtime bloat.
- Cloud Sync: Optional backup and cross-device sync using your native Chrome profile.
- Export & Import: Effortlessly backup and restore your entire configuration as a JSON file.
